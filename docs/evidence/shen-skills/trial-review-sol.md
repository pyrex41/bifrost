The three actual parsers check semantic actions rather than trusting a declared parse-result type. Account transitions and intentionally stateful counters are properly distinguished. |

Terra-original additionally discloses its untyped low-level counter. Revised graph/counter bodies are checked. **All three actual parsers add number-predicate and conditional verified-evidence rules:** acceptance is relative to these trusted logical bridges, not a trust-free result. Concrete datatype rules, stable built-ins and runtime behavior remain assumptions everywhere. |
